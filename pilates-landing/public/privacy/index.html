Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Let's Pilates LA ("we", "us") runs a Pilates and Gyrotonic studio at 672 S La Fayette Park Pl, STE 674, Los Angeles, CA 90057. This policy explains what information we collect from clients and visitors and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Your name, phone number and email address when you book a session, sign up for a training course or contact us.
  • Your appointments, payments and notes our instructors need to teach you safely.

How we use it

  • To schedule and run your sessions, take payments and answer your questions.
  • To send appointment text messages: reminders, schedule changes and cancellations.

Text messages (SMS)

When you give us your mobile number while booking, you agree to receive appointment text messages from Let's Pilates LA. Message frequency varies with your bookings. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop receiving texts and HELP for help. You can also call us at 310-995-0046.

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or your text messaging consent with anyone for marketing. Your number is shared only with our text message provider (Twilio) to deliver our messages.

How we protect it

Client information is stored in a secure, access-controlled system that only our staff can use. We keep it only as long as we need it to run the studio and meet our legal duties.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time by calling 310-995-0046 or messaging us on Instagram @Letspilates_la.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new date.
